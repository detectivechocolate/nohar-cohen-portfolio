Archive of Unknown Places

Archive of Unknown Places is an ongoing archive centered on a place with an uncertain history. Through architectural drawings, occupation records, fragments, and research materials, the project assembles a history that tells a story of space lived through generations.

The archive remains deliberately unfinished. Instead of safeguarding a static past, the project treats the archive as an evolving entity—its history constantly reshaped through new additions, comparisons, and reinterpretations.
