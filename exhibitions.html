About

Nohar Cohen's work engages with history and memory. Born in 1999 and raised in Israel, she is of Jewish Yemeni origin and draws inspiration from her identity and the different cultures and traditions each society creates, past and present.

Her vibrant palette is inspired by Middle Eastern scenery, from the Negev Desert to the greenery of the Lower Galilee.
